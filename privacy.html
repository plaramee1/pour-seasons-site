Privacy Policy

Pour Seasons · last updated October 7, 2026

Pour Seasons is a puzzle game by Patrick Laramée. This page explains what data the game uses. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to show them.

What the game keeps

Your progress (levels, stars, scenes, streak) and settings are stored only on your device. If you turn on iCloud, Apple syncs them between your own devices. We never receive them. There's no account and no sign-in, and we don't run analytics or ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows occasional full-screen ads between levels, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's software may collect a device identifier for advertising, an approximate location derived from your IP address, information about the ads you see and tap, and crash and performance data. The game does not ask for permission to track you across other apps and websites, so Google can't use Apple's advertising identifier for that.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Union and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before ads use your data. You can change your choice any time under Privacy choices on the home screen. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases and codes

The optional "Ad-free" purchase and gift codes are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and no personal information. We only see aggregate sales reports from Apple.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and isn't directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
