Pour Seasons — Support

Pour sand between jars until every jar holds one colour. Every level can be solved, undo is unlimited, and the hint button (the lightbulb) is free.

I'm stuck

Tap the lightbulb for the next move, or Undo. Each level also has one free spare jar (the + button). If the game says there's no way through, undo a few moves.

Removing ads

Tap Remove ads at the top of the home screen. It's a one-time purchase that works on all your devices and with Family Sharing. On a new device, open the same sheet and tap Restore.

I have a code

Open Remove ads → Redeem a code, or in the App Store app tap your profile picture → Redeem Gift Card or Code. Ads turn off as soon as the code is accepted.

Stars and scenes

Every new level you clear earns a star for the season you're playing, and the daily puzzle earns two. Stars decorate that season's scene. Finish a scene to unlock its jar and sand.
